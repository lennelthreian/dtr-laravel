Biometrics Time Logging — Allowable Times
1

How Your Time is Recorded

Your daily attendance is captured through the ZKTeco Biometrics Device. Each time you authenticate using the facial recognition feature, the system records a punch timestamp. These punches are automatically classified as Time In, Break Out, Break In, or Time Out based on their time of day relative to the official work schedule.

2

The table below shows the standard schedule for each work-week type and the allowed flexi-time windows. Flexi-time is a grace period during which you can punch without being marked Late or Undertime.

3

Lunch Break Deduction

A 60-minute lunch break is automatically deducted from your total hours worked. This deduction is applied between your AM Arrival (Time In) and PM Departure (Time Out). There is no need to punch separately for lunch — your Break Out and Break In punches record the gap.

4

Important Reminders

  • Punching after the flexi grace period will mark you as Late.
  • Punching out before the flexi grace period will mark you as Undertime.
  • Always record all four (4) required entries: Time In, Break Out, Break In, and Time Out.
  • Missed punches will cause missing or incomplete entries in your DTR. You may request a Time Correction edit to fix them.
  • Biometrics data syncs automatically between the device and the e-DTR system.
How to View & Manage Your Daily Time Record
1

Open the e-DTR Records Page

From the sidebar, click "e-DTR Records" or go to:

http://192.168.1.151:8000/dtr
2

Select Month and Year

Use the Month and Year dropdowns to choose which period you want to view.

3

Click "Generate DTR"

Your Daily Time Record for the selected month will appear showing AM/PM in/out times, total hours worked, and any remarks (Late, Undertime, Absent, etc.).

4

Print or Save as PDF

Once your DTR is displayed, click the "Print / Save PDF" button in the top bar. Use your browser's print dialog to save as PDF or print on paper.

5

Adjust Print Scale to Fit the Paper

When the print dialog opens, configure these settings so the DTR fits on the page:

1. Set Destination to your printer (e.g. "EPSON L3210", "HP LaserJet") or "Save as PDF"
2. Set Layout to Landscape
3. Set Margins to None or Minimum
4. Under More settings, set Scale to 70-80% (adjust until the DTR fits on one page)
5. Check "Background graphics" so colors and badges appear
6. Click Print or Save
How to Toggle Between 4-Day & 5-Day Work Week
1

What is the Work Week Toggle?

Employees may be assigned a 4-Day or 5-Day work week schedule. A 4-day week has longer daily hours (10 hrs/day) while a 5-day week follows the standard 8 hrs/day. You can override the default work week per day or for the entire month.

2

Toggle a Single Day

On your DTR table, each day row shows a badge labeled "4d" or "5d". Click this badge to switch that specific day's work week type between 4-day and 5-day. The total hours for that day will recalculate accordingly.

3

Toggle the Global Default

The global default work week (4-Day or 5-Day) is set by the Super Admin / HR Account. Individual employees can still override specific days using the per-day badge toggle on their DTR.

4

Per-Day Overrides Take Priority

If you toggle individual days, those per-day settings override the global default. Untoggled days will follow the global setting. Changes are applied immediately.

How to Submit DTR Edit Requests
1

Generate Your DTR First

Go to the DTR page, select your month/year, and click "Generate DTR" so the table is displayed.

2

Click the Edit (Pencil) Icon

Each day row has a pencil icon. Click it to open the Edit Request form for that date.

3

Choose the Type of Edit

Select from the following edit types:

  • Time Correction (AM/PM in/out)
  • Absent (whole day or half-day)
  • On Leave (with leave hours)
  • Work From Home (WFH)
  • Special Order (with SO number)
  • Travel Order (with TO number)
  • Official Business (with OB number)
  • Locator Slip (Official/Personal)
4

Provide a Reason & Submit

Always include a reason in the text area. Click "Submit Request". Your edit request will be forwarded to your supervisor for approval.

5

Track Your Requests

Approved edits immediately update your DTR. Pending requests are shown to you and your supervisor. Rejected requests appear in a table below your DTR with the rejection reason.
