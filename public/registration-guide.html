How to View & Manage Your Daily Time Record
1

Open the e-DTR Records Page

From the sidebar, click "e-DTR Records" or go to:

http://192.168.1.151:8000/dtr
2

Select Month and Year

Use the Month and Year dropdowns to choose which period you want to view.

3

Click "Generate DTR"

Your Daily Time Record for the selected month will appear showing AM/PM in/out times, total hours worked, and any remarks (Late, Undertime, Absent, etc.).

4

Print or Save as PDF

Once your DTR is displayed, click the "Print / Save PDF" button in the top bar. Use your browser's print dialog to save as PDF or print on paper.

5

Adjust Print Scale to Fit the Paper

When the print dialog opens, configure these settings so the DTR fits on the page:

1. Set Destination to your printer (e.g. "EPSON L3210", "HP LaserJet") or "Save as PDF"
2. Set Layout to Landscape
3. Set Margins to None or Minimum
4. Under More settings, set Scale to 70-80% (adjust until the DTR fits on one page)
5. Check "Background graphics" so colors and badges appear
6. Click Print or Save
How to Submit DTR Edit Requests
1

Generate Your DTR First

Go to the DTR page, select your month/year, and click "Generate DTR" so the table is displayed.

2

Click the Edit (Pencil) Icon

Each day row has a pencil icon. Click it to open the Edit Request form for that date.

3

Choose the Type of Edit

Select from the following edit types:

  • Time Correction (AM/PM in/out)
  • Absent (whole day or half-day)
  • On Leave (with leave hours)
  • Work From Home (WFH)
  • Special Order (with SO number)
  • Travel Order (with TO number)
  • Official Business (with OB number)
  • Locator Slip (Official/Personal)
4

Provide a Reason & Submit

Always include a reason in the text area. Click "Submit Request". Your edit request will be forwarded to your supervisor for approval.

5

Track Your Requests

Approved edits immediately update your DTR. Pending requests are shown to you and your supervisor. Rejected requests appear in a table below your DTR with the rejection reason.

How to Edit Your Profile & Change Password
1

Go to My Profile

From the sidebar, click "My Profile" or go to:

http://192.168.1.151:8000/profile
2

Update Your Details

You can edit your name, sex, email, position, and honorific prefix/suffix. All fields except middle name and honorifics are required. Click "Update Profile" to save.

3

Change Your Password

Click the "Change Password" link. Enter your current password, new password (min. 8 characters), and confirm. Click "Change Password" to save.

4

Return to DTR

Use the sidebar navigation or the "Back to Profile" link to return to the DTR system.
